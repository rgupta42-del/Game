What do Rating / Peak / Fit mean? Rating = career build-around value (talent over a full 15-yr career, lightly adjusted for longevity, durability & intangibles). Active players carry an unfinished-career discount — the less they've played and banked (accolades, titles, status if it ended today), and the higher their remaining injury risk, the bigger the haircut — so a completed legend outranks an equal-talent projection. Peak = best-self ability. Fit = how much this pick helps your roster right now (rating + filling open needs − redundancy).
